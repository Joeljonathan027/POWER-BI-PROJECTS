This dashboard displays an analysis of mental health impacts related to remote work based on a survey of 5,000 employees. Key insights include: 
Burnout is identified as the most frequently occurring mental health condition among remote employees.
Employees in the IT industry report the highest total hours worked per week, making it the most demanding industry.
The average age of employees surveyed is 41 years, and the highest reported years of experience is 35 years.
The data indicates a need for targeted initiatives such as workload management and wellness workshops, particularly for high-demand sectors like IT.
